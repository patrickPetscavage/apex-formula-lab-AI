# Build 3 priorities
1. Obtain an author-verified, redistributable historical assembly; inspect actual topology and hierarchy before importing. Replace reconstructed body panels and schematic internals while retaining stable IDs. Seek drawings and dimensional validation, not just more polygons.
2. Validate materials and all camera angles on a WebGL-capable laptop. Profile draw calls and frame times; introduce mesh LOD and shared materials where measured performance warrants them.
3. Obtain surveyed Suzuka elevation/banking, boundary geometry, period configuration and verified sector positions. Replace estimated terrain and scenery with licensed accurate models.
4. Calibrate effective power, drag/downforce, tire load sensitivity and combined slip to defensible data. Add vertical-curve forces and suspension before claiming engineering fidelity.
5. Replace the implemented bounded geometric smoothing with constrained time-optimal trajectory generation, retaining saved offsets and same-line comparison controls. Import observed modern telemetry only with event/lap metadata, alignment error and rights provenance.
6. Add mechanical mount constraints, parametric shape editing and validated component mass budgets; then export formats and CAD/solver integration. Every-fastener completeness remains a long-term goal.
