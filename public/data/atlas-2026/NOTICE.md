# 2026 circuit atlas: sources, rights and accuracy

Horizontal roads, pit lanes and structure footprints are derived from **OpenStreetMap**, © OpenStreetMap contributors, under the [Open Database License 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/). See [OSM attribution and copyright](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). The derived database is offered under ODbL 1.0; individual JSON files include the original way/structure IDs and acquisition date. No official circuit CAD or proprietary game assets are distributed.

Elevation is sampled through [Open Topo Data](https://www.opentopodata.org/datasets/srtm/) from NASA/USGS SRTM Version 3, native 3-arc-second/approximately 90 m resolution. The cached terrain grid is **coarser than the native dataset**: each file records its actual grid spacing. Orthometric heights use EGM96; local +Y is that height minus the retained local datum. This is a coarse landscape/road-height proxy, not surveyed road elevation, bridge deck height, banking, or current construction geometry. NASA SRTM source data is openly available; Open Topo Data is credited as the retrieval service. No elevation-service credentials are included.

Monaco, Madring, Baku, Marina Bay and Las Vegas use OSM street coordinates selected/projected using the MIT-licensed [Tomislav Bacinger F1 circuit route guides](https://github.com/bacinger/f1-circuits). The guides are unofficial and acknowledge an initial Google My Maps source; their coordinate arrays and imagery are **not redistributed here**. The published coordinates are projections onto separately acquired OSM roads. Route selection remains provisional. The guide use and measured matching residuals are recorded in each relevant file, and the guide's MIT notice is supplied alongside this file. Do not infer homologation or survey precision from a small matching residual.

Calendar names/dates and circuit references were checked against [F1](https://www.formula1.com/en/racing/2026) and [FIA](https://www.fia.com/events/fia-formula-one-world-championship/season-2026/2026-fia-formula-one-world-championship) on 11 November 2026. The current calendar contains 23 venues; the Bahrain event is at Sepang and the Saudi Arabian event is canceled. These are geographic/calendar references, not 2025+ session observations.

Procedural asphalt, grass, gravel, trees, lighting and architectural materials are original artistic assets. Widths, unmapped kerbs, vegetation, missing structure heights and surface details are estimates. A modeled pit-lane marking does not implement pit-stop physics. Suzuka's approximately 8 m bridge separation is an explicit estimate. Each asset includes warnings for changed or incomplete layout information, approximate start/finish placement, missing banking and direction review.

Detailed geometry is loaded one selected circuit at a time. The original three engineering circuits retain their existing numerical data and versions; the new atlas has separate fingerprinted track/model identities. See `docs/GRAPHICS_2026.md` and `docs/2026_CIRCUIT_INVENTORY.md` in the source repository.
